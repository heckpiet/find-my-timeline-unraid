Privacy & access

Location history and Apple sessions require careful protection.

  • Keep the WebUI on a trusted network or behind an authenticated HTTPS proxy.
  • Back up both persistent appdata paths and protect those backups.
  • Use a unique WebUI administrator password that is different from the Apple ID password.
